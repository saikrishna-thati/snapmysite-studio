## Music Stems
Music stems are designed to be replaced with licensed/royalty-free music for production use.

### Placeholder Music System
The current music system uses procedural generation and oscillator-based sounds for development purposes. For production deployment, replace with:

#### Recommended Music Sources
- **Epidemic Sound** - Subscription-based royalty-free music with stem separation
- **Artlist** - High-quality royalty-free music for commercial use
- **Musicbed** - Professional music licensing for film and video
- **PremiumBeat** - Royalty-free music with simple licensing
- **AudioJungle** - Budget-friendly royalty-free music options

### Music License Requirements
- Commercial use license for product launch films
- Stem separation capability for structured mixing
- No attribution required for professional use
- Worldwide distribution rights
- Perpetual usage rights

### Integration Notes
- Music should be provided as separate stems: full, drums, bass, melody, harmony
- BPM detection for synchronization with storyboard timing
- Key information for harmonic compatibility
- Loop points for seamless editing

## Text-to-Speech Services
Voiceover generation uses browser-based Web Speech API for development. For production, integrate with:

#### Recommended TTS Services
- **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech** - High-quality neural voices
- **Amazon Polly** - Natural-sounding AI voices
- **Microsoft Azure Speech** - Enterprise-grade TTS
- **ElevenLabs** - Ultra-realistic AI voice synthesis

### TTS License Considerations
- Character limits and pricing tiers
- Commercial use rights for voice cloning
- Voice ownership and redistribution rights
- Attribution requirements for voice samples

## Audio Processing Libraries
- **Web Audio API** - Native browser audio processing (no license required)
- **GSAP** - Animation library for audio timing (licensed for commercial use)
- **Three.js** - 3D audio spatialization (MIT license)

## Custom Audio Managers
The following custom audio managers are part of the Snapmy.site Film Engine v2:

### Music Manager (`js/music-manager.js`)
- Professional music stem management
- Storyboard-aware music cutting and structuring
- LUFS normalization (-14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP)
- Stem mixing and automation
- BPM synchronization and loop positioning

### SFX Manager (`js/sfx-manager.js`)
- Layered sound effects system
- Stereo placement and spatialization
- Ducking and sidechain compression
- Bus-based audio routing
- Storyboard-integrated SFX generation

### Voiceover Manager (`js/voiceover-manager.js`)
- TTS integration with claims.json
- Voiceover script generation
- Music ducking for voice regions
- Professional timing and pacing
- Multi-voice support

## License Compliance
- All SFX samples are CC0 (public domain) - no attribution required
- Music stems must be replaced with properly licensed content for production
- TTS services require appropriate commercial licensing
- Custom audio managers are proprietary to Snapmy.site

## Audio Quality Standards
- Sample rate: 44.1kHz (CD quality)
- Bit depth: 16-bit or 24-bit
- Target loudness: -14 LUFS integrated
- Max true peak: -1 dBTP
- Format: WAV for mastering, MP3 for distribution
